Join Us

Bring a team, join as a solo player, or help us run the next event.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below with your team or player details
  2. We match you to an open league, division, or event
  3. You get a confirmation email with dates, venue, and fees

Before you write to us

  • Applications are reviewed every Monday
  • Teams of any size can register — we'll help you find the right division
  • No full squad? Join our pick-up games as a solo player
  • Replies usually take two to three working days
