About TEAMO

Grassroots football, organized right.

Our story

Open Pitch started in 2019 as three friends who got tired of chasing down a pitch, a referee, and eleven other players just to have a kickabout. What began as a single Sunday five-a-side game turned into a proper booking system, then a league, then a full events calendar covering half the city.

We don't run a team of our own — we run the football around it. Pitches, referees, schedules, score sheets, and the odd smoke machine for a big final: we handle the logistics so teams, players, and fans can just show up and play.

What we stand for

  1. Football should be easy to organize, not just to play
  2. Every match gets a proper pitch and a qualified referee
  3. Transparent fees — no hidden costs, ever
  4. Open to any team, any age group, any skill level

Our mission

We exist so that organizing a football match takes five minutes, not five phone calls. Every event on our calendar comes with a booked pitch, a referee, and a clear schedule — teams just turn up and play.
